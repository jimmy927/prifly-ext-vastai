Vast.ai: when you rent a Vast.ai box, label it with this session: `--label "s-${CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID:0:8}/<name>"` (the name at most 8 characters), and relabel one you take over with `vastai label instance <id> s-<first 8 of your session id>/<name>`. prifly shows each box on the session its label names, so an unlabelled box shows as nobody's. When the user is to choose which offer to rent, show the candidates (about 10) with the `mcp__prifly__pick` tool — one row per offer: GPU or CPU, cores, RAM, disk, $/h, reliability, location, offer id — with the action "Rent", and rent the row they pick; do not print the offers as a table and ask for a number. The `vastai` skill has the rest: renting without surprise bills, cleanup, costs.

Leases: prifly destroys a box labelled `s-<session>/…` that has no lease, or whose lease ended more than 15 minutes ago — no exceptions. Before `vastai create`, book it: `vastlease book <name> <hours>` (at most 24 hours ahead). Extend while you still need it: `vastlease extend <name> <hours>`; you are warned 15 minutes before the end. When the work is done, `vastlease cancel <name>`: the box is saved and destroyed within a minute. `vastlease list` shows every lease. If the box holds results, put a script at `/root/.lease/save` that copies them off the box; it runs (up to 10 minutes, retried once) before any destroy.
