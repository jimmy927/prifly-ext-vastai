Vast.ai: every box you rent carries the label `<owner>/s-<first 8 of your session id>/<name>` (the name at most 8 characters), e.g. `jimmy/s-2b89f308/lc-box1`. Do not build it yourself: `vastlease book <name> <hours>` prints the exact `--label` to rent with, and the owner is this prifly's (its extension config, else `$USER`). Relabel a box you take over with `vastai label instance <id> <that label>`. prifly shows each box on the session its label names; a box without such a label shows as nobody's, and one with another owner belongs to another prifly on the same account. When the user is to choose which offer to rent, show the candidates (about 10) with the `mcp__prifly__pick` tool — one row per offer: GPU or CPU, cores, RAM, disk, $/h, reliability, location, offer id — with the action "Rent", and rent the row they pick; do not print the offers as a table and ask for a number. The `vastai` skill has the rest: renting without surprise bills, cleanup, costs.

Leases: prifly destroys a box labelled `<owner>/s-<session>/…` with its own owner (or the older `s-<session>/…`) that has no lease, or whose lease ended more than 15 minutes ago — no exceptions. Boxes of another owner, or of a session this prifly does not know, are never destroyed. Before `vastai create`, book it: `vastlease book <name> <hours>` (at most 24 hours ahead), and rent with the `--label` it prints. Extend while you still need it: `vastlease extend <name> <hours>`; you are warned 15 minutes before the end. When the work is done, `vastlease cancel <name>`: the box is saved and destroyed within a minute. Each command takes the name, the whole label, or the box id. `vastlease list` shows every lease. If the box holds results, put a script at `/root/.lease/save` that copies them off the box; it runs (up to 10 minutes, retried once) before any destroy.
