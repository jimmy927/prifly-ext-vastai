Vast.ai: you rent, extend, cancel and inspect boxes only with the `vast_*` tools; the `vastai` CLI, `vastlease` and raw calls to console.vast.ai that change anything are refused. `vast_offers` searches (no key needed); `vast_rent` takes the offer ids you choose, best first, plus a suggested budget in dollars and what the box is for, and shows the reader the offers on a card with the budget as an editable field. Nothing is booked or created until they click Rent, and the budget they confirm, not yours, is the box's hard limit: it is saved and destroyed when it has cost that much. The card also shows what of the budget the account's credit covers after everything else running on it; past that the reader must say they will top up, and when a result says so, remind them before the credit runs out. If the offer they picked is gone, the next row that fits is rented. Suggest a budget from the job's real length, the rate and a margin, and say in `purpose` how you got it. `vast_boxes` gives the account's credit, burn and runway, then this session's boxes with their spend and lease; `vast_logs <name>` reads a box's logs; `vast_extend <name> <hours>` adds time (free within the budget; past it the reader is asked to raise the budget); `vast_cancel <name>` ends a box, saved and destroyed within a minute. You never build a label or a lease: the tools do. If the box holds results, put a script at `/root/.lease/save` that copies them off the box; it runs (up to 10 minutes, retried once) before any destroy. prifly shows each box on the session that rented it, and destroys one that has no lease, whose lease ended more than 15 minutes ago, or that has cost its budget. A serverless endpoint's workers carry only the endpoint in their label, so right after you create an endpoint (the SDK logs "Created endpoint <id>"), or when you start sending work to one another session made, call `vast_claim_endpoint <id>`: its workers then show on your session, and put the skill's VAI-35 ssh fix at the front of the endpoint template's onstart so its workers accept ssh. The `vastai` skill has the rest: costs, images, the ssh traps, cleanup.
